Surface 3 · the under-18 exit

Replaces the modal body when “I am under 18” is tapped. She answered honestly and the screen has to earn that. No scolding, no lock-out language, no dead end.

Thank you for telling us.

BioWellth is for adults 18 and older, so we are not able to keep going. We will sign you out now.

If you would like, we can delete the data on this account first. You can also ask us to do that later at support@mybiowellth.com.

Sign out is primary, deletion is secondary. Deletion is the better outcome for her and for us under DPDP, but making it the primary button pressures a minor into an irreversible action at the worst possible moment. Neutral default, deletion clearly offered, and a durable route by email if she wants to think about it.
Sequencing constraint, found while designing this. delete-account derives the target uid from the caller’s JWT, so it needs a LIVE session. Signing out first makes deletion impossible. The order must be delete, then sign out, never the reverse. “Delete my data and sign out” therefore routes into the EXISTING typed-confirm delete flow already wired at dashboard.html:2047, and the sign-out happens as its consequence. It is not a one-tap delete.
Why reuse the typed confirm. The existing flow asks her to type to confirm. That is friction at an uncomfortable moment, but deletion is irreversible and this is the place a mis-tap costs most. Reusing it also means there is no second deletion path to keep correct.
